Who I am

I'm a multidisciplinary digital creative working across UX/UI design, audiovisual production, and digital content. My background combines design thinking with hands-on production, allowing me to move comfortably between strategy, visual systems, and execution.

Over the last few years I’ve worked across football media, digital products, branding projects, and creative production environments. From filming matchday content to designing interfaces and building websites, I’ve learned to adapt quickly and think across disciplines.

How I work

What to expect working with me

01
I start by understanding context
I focus on the goal, audience, and constraints before producing anything. In fast-moving environments like sports and content production, clarity upfront prevents rework later.
02
I work between exploration and execution
I move from rough ideas to structured outputs quickly, whether it's Figma prototypes, video edits, or content templates. Iteration happens through making, not endless discussion.
03
I stay close to production reality
My background in audiovisual production means I think about feasibility, timing, and delivery from the start — not just the concept.
04
I keep files and assets usable
Structured Figma files, organised exports, and clear naming conventions so work can be picked up by anyone without explanation.
05
I take responsibility for outcomes
I don’t just deliver tasks — I try to improve the result. If something can be adjusted or improved, I bring it up and propose alternatives.
06
I adapt to fast-changing environments
From football clubs to academic projects and product work, I’m used to shifting priorities, tight deadlines, and mixed team structures.
