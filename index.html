About

Multidisciplinary by design

I'm a multidisciplinary digital creative with a background in UX/UI design, audiovisual media, and digital content creation. I enjoy working across design, video, and web, combining visual thinking with practical problem solving.

My experience ranges from writing scrips, productios, editing and content production to interface design and front-end basics, allowing me to adapt to different types of creative and digital projects.
